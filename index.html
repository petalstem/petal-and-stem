Our story

The days started to blur together. I spent most of my time applying for jobs and wondering what would come next. After being unemployed longer than I expected, I wanted to do something meaningful with my time.

The idea of starting a business came slowly. If I couldn't find an opportunity in the corporate world, I thought, why not create one myself?

I've always loved flowers, so making handmade bouquets felt like a natural choice. Instead of constantly looking for a job, I was finally creating something of my own.

It wasn't a big business, and orders didn't come every day. Most came during graduations, holidays, and special occasions. But seeing someone happy with a bouquet I made made it all worth it.

I realized I wasn't just filling my time. I had found something I genuinely enjoyed, and that gave me a sense of purpose again.

Handmade with love in Lupon, Petal and Stem creates floral keepsakes for people who want their flowers to last beyond the moment.
